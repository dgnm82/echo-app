Tidelings (ocean Echo family) is a proposal. Roster (8): sea_otter, seal, dolphin, sea_turtle, penguin, octopus, seahorse, hermit_crab (manatee out).
All 8 swim in the ocean mini-games. At home otter, seal and penguin walk and the hermit crab scuttles; in the games the hermit crab drifts/bobs on a current with its shell.
These 4 cycles (dolphin, sea_turtle, seahorse swim; hermit_crab drift-on-current) are 48x48 x 6 frames @ 12 fps (2 ticks each), facing right, for the title tidepool look test.
Sprite pixels are never tinted.
Dolphin is drawn round and friendly (soft rounded fin, smile), never shark-like.
Not referenced by px/title/title_frames.json and not part of any firmware pack. Do not ship.

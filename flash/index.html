3.5-inch board: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5-C

Care/home preview (413bd29) for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5-C. Production linked; matching engine booted in QEMU with HAL stubs; not verified on a physical board.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop (not Safari, Firefox, a phone or a tablet).
  2. Plug a USB-C data cable into the board's only USB-C port. Charge-only cables don't work: if nothing shows up, try another cable.
  3. Click a button below, then Connect, and pick the port called USB JTAG/serial debug unit (or "ESP32-S3").
  4. Won't connect? Put the board in download mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT, then click Connect again. (Don't hold PWR: holding it about 6 seconds switches the board off.)
  5. Installing takes a few minutes. Keep the cable in until it says "Installation complete". If the screen doesn't come on by itself, tap RESET (or unplug and plug back in).

Recommended: UPDATE — care/home preview. Four parts update the bootloader, partition table, app and core assets. No part or sector touches saves or NVS. Existing child, Echo, music and mission data are retained; no erase prompt. A successful new Echo clears its old memory book as on the phone. In-firmware Echo-only delete keeps the child and music.

Preview, not complete phone parity: no games or mission offers (data kept). Book/release/recorded sound and authored visuals remain incomplete. Music/quiet/chirp/3D-den/stay/shared controls are read-only preview information, not working switches. See build evidence, gaps and checksums.

Starting fresh: "Start fresh" installs the same version and erases all child and Echo saves (including child music, names, progress and memory book), so onboarding starts again. This is not the firmware's Echo-only delete. Only use it to erase everything; it can't be undone.

2-inch board: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2

v6 test build (c265026) for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2, v6 streamlined case (touch + Power + speaker + haptic)

Touch only: hold the × in the top-right about 1 second to quit a game. Volume and Test buzz are in Grown-Ups. Flashing erases saves.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial; not Safari, Firefox or a phone).
  2. Plug the handheld in with a data USB-C cable (the port on the top edge).
  3. Click Connect and pick the port called USB JTAG/serial debug unit (or "ESP32-S3").
  4. First time: say yes to erase. Wait for "Installation complete", then unplug and plug back in.
This browser can't flash devices. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure page (https). Open the GitHub Pages address, not a local file.

3.5C touch timing without UART: hold Lock on Home or Origin for 3 seconds, release, solve the multiplication gate, tap Next to Touch timing and open it. Values refresh every 5 seconds; software timing, not measured finger-to-pixel latency.

2-inch after flashing: finish onboarding, then open Grown-Ups for Volume, Test buzz and Device test.

Not connecting, blank screen or no sound? See the flash and troubleshooting note.
